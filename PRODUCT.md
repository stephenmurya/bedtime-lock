# Bedtime Lock

register: product

## Users

One person who wants a reliable, private, difficult-to-bypass bedtime boundary on their Android phone. They configure it once, usually in a calm moment during the day, then glance at it briefly before bed or after waking.

## Product Purpose

Bedtime Lock turns a chosen sleep schedule into a native Android enforcement session. It blocks distracting apps while keeping essential phone and clock functions available, with a partner-controlled temporary access path.

## Tone

Quiet, clear, accountable, mature, and non-judgmental. The product should feel like a trusted personal utility, not parental control software or a gamified wellness app.

## Anti-goals

- No streaks, analytics, charts, accounts, network dependency, or social features.
- No decorative dashboards, neon, gradients, or aggressive warnings.
- Never claim that normal Accessibility-based enforcement is impossible to bypass.
