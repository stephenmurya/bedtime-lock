# Bedtime Lock design notes

## Scene

One person configures the app in a dim bedroom at night, focused and slightly tired, so the interface uses a deep warm charcoal surface with low visual noise and a single muted amber accent.

## Color strategy

Restrained. Tinted charcoal neutrals carry most of the surface. Amber is reserved for active schedule states, primary actions, and attention moments. Soft sage marks healthy permission status.

## Typography

Geist-like system sans, with a strong size and weight hierarchy. Schedule times are the visual anchor. Supporting copy stays short and readable.

## Shape and rhythm

Generous vertical spacing, low-radius ForUI surfaces, hairline separators only when useful, and no nested card stacks. Use ForUI controls for the interaction vocabulary.

## Motion

Short, functional fades and subtle state changes. No page-load choreography or bounce.
